## 📌 Project Overview

Customer retention is one of the most important factors for business growth. This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns and identifying the key factors that influence customer decisions.

The **Customer Retention & Churn Analysis Dashboard** was developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights. The dashboard helps businesses understand why customers leave and provides actionable insights to improve retention strategies.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze customer churn behavior
- Identify factors contributing to customer loss
- Understand customer retention patterns
- Compare churn across different customer segments
- Create an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

# 📊 Dashboard Features

## 1. Customer Overview KPIs

The dashboard provides high-level customer metrics:

- 👥 Total Customers
- ❌ Churned Customers
- ✅ Retained Customers

---

## 2. Churn Analysis

Analyzed customer churn based on:

### Contract Type
- Identifies which contract customers have higher churn risk.

### Payment Method
- Shows payment methods associated with increased churn.

### Internet Service
- Compares churn behavior across different service categories.

---

## 3. Customer Behavior Analysis

Includes:

### Customer Churn Distribution

Shows the percentage of customers who:
- Stayed with the company
- Left the company

### Customer Lifetime vs Monthly Charges

Analyzes the relationship between customer tenure and monthly spending behavior.

---

# 💡 Key Business Insights

📌 **26.5% of customers have churned**, highlighting a need for stronger retention strategies.

📌 **Month-to-month contract customers show the highest churn rate**, suggesting that long-term contracts improve retention.

📌 **Electronic check users have higher churn risk**, indicating payment experience may influence customer satisfaction.

📌 **Fiber optic customers contribute significantly to churn**, requiring further service quality analysis.

📌 **Long-term customers demonstrate better retention**, showing the importance of customer loyalty programs.
